====================================================================
  PJESA E ERDONES - MACHINE LEARNING (dosja /ml)
====================================================================

Erdona ka ndertuar pipeline-in e plote te Inteligjences Artificiale
per parashikimin e diabetit, duke perdorur dataset-in Pima Indians.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
  SKEDARET E PUNUAR (9 skripta Python + 1 Notebook)
--------------------------------------------------------------------

  00_generate_datasets.py    - Gjeneron dataset-in (768 pacient)
  01_inspect_datasets.py     - Inspekton null, zero, balancen
  02_preprocessing.py        - Pastron te dhenat (median + IQR),
                               ndan 80/20 dhe skalon me StandardScaler
  03_train_models.py         - Trajnon 5 modele:
                                 * kNN
                                 * Random Forest
                                 * Logistic Regression
                                 * MLP Arkitektura 1 (64)
                                 * MLP Arkitektura 2 (128,64,32)
  04_kmeans_clustering.py    - K-Means K=3 (Rrezik Ulet/Mesatar/Larte)
  05_predict.py              - Teston parashikimin per nje pacient
  06_visualizations.py       - Confusion Matrix, PCA, krahasime
  07_hyperparameter_tuning.py- GridSearchCV per te 4 modelet
  08_cross_validation.py     - Stratified 5-Fold CV
  healthconnect_ml.py/.ipynb - Pipeline i konsoliduar akademik

--------------------------------------------------------------------
  REZULTATET (dosja /models)
--------------------------------------------------------------------

  - 10 modele te ruajtura (.pkl): 5 baseline + 4 tuned + 1 K-Means
  - diabetes_production_model.pkl -> modeli me i mire (F1)
  - 4 CSV me rezultate te vleresimit

--------------------------------------------------------------------
  RRJEDHA
--------------------------------------------------------------------

  diabetes.csv -> Inspektim -> Pastrim -> Split 80/20 -> Skalim
       -> Trajnim (Supervised + Unsupervised)
       -> Cross-Validation -> Hyperparameter Tuning
       -> Vizualizime -> Modeli Final per Backend
